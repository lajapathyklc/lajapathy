End-to-end product design & UX strategy | Fintech | Mobile platform

E-Wallet Platform for P2P Payments & Financial Insights

A simpler way to move money, understand spending, and feel confident about every transaction.

10M+ annual transactions supported25% faster transaction completion

The
Problem

Users struggled to complete onboarding and transactions because financial tasks were fragmented across apps, unclear fees, and unreliable flows.

  • Too many steps for onboarding and payment flows
  • Unclear confirmation and transaction status
  • Inconsistent experiences across payment methods
  • Limited awareness of spending habits and financial health

The core issue wasn’t just usability. It was a lack of trust and clarity in financial interactions.

Key
Insights

Users don’t struggle with complexity; they hesitate because unclear system feedback makes financial actions feel risky and unreliable.

My Role &
Ownership

Led end-to-end UX strategy and product design, owning:

  • Problem framing and UX direction
  • User flows, interaction design, and system thinking
  • High-fidelity UI and design system alignment
  • Collaboration with product and engineering teams

Constraints &
Complexity

  • Multi-transaction flows with high accuracy requirements
  • Need for speed vs. trust balance in transactions
  • Complex data visibility across accounts and payments
  • Ensuring consistent patterns across onboarding, wallet, and transfers

Key Product &
UX Decisions

  • Simplified onboarding using progressive disclosure to reduce cognitive load
  • Introduced real-time system feedback for transaction status (processing, success, failure)
  • Used clear, predictable actions for critical payment steps
  • Reduced friction in payment flows by restructuring high-intent interactions
  • Standardized transaction patterns to ensure consistency and predictability
  • Designed clear visual hierarchy to improve readability of financial data

Tradeoffs &
Decisions

  • Chose progressive disclosure over full visibility, reducing overwhelm while aiding one-step clarity
  • Prioritized speed over dense interactions to improve user trust
  • Simplified financial data presentation without losing depth
  • Balanced UI components with custom variations to improve scalability and consistency

User Flow
Transformation

Redesigned the end-to-end flow to eliminate friction, improve clarity, and embed confirmation checkpoints across critical steps.

  • Mapped the end-to-end user journey to identify drop-offs
  • Introduced clear and progressive decision points in high-risk feedback
  • Simplified navigation and interaction patterns
  • Validated flows through rapid prototyping

Results &
Impact

The redesign improved conversion, reduced transaction time, and increased user trust across critical financial workflows.

  • 33% increase in onboarding conversion

  • Improved confidence through clearer system feedback
  • Reduced errors and support dependency
  • Improved engagement with financial insights
