GoCart: A Scalable Commerce Experience

A modular, data-informed commerce experience built to make discovery, evaluation, and purchase clearer across the customer journey.

Discovery & checkoutScalable UX systems

The
Problem

Despite a rich catalog and frequent campaigns, users faced fragmented navigation, effortful decisions, and inconsistently prioritized actions.

  • Discovery and checkout drop-offs
  • Low engagement with campaigns
  • Repeated patterns were redesigned inconsistently

Key
Insights

The core issue was workflow fragmentation, not a lack of features.

  • Decision points create the most friction
  • Consistent layouts help users scan and compare
  • Predictable interaction patterns make the platform easier to extend

My Role &
Ownership

Led UX across research, workflow design, interaction modeling, and UI execution, while aligning product, engineering, and marketing.

  • Framed the problem at a system level
  • Translated business objectives into experience principles
  • Facilitated reviews to resolve trade-offs early
  • Balanced customer clarity, technical feasibility, and delivery timelines

Key Product &
UX Decisions

  • Structured search, filters, and navigation for faster discovery
  • Created consistent patterns across product details, cart, and checkout
  • Reduced steps between discovery and purchase intent
  • Used progressive disclosure and predictable primary actions
  • Designed reusable patterns for campaigns and seasonal traffic

User Flow
Transformation

Redesigned connected workflows rather than isolated screens, clarifying task progression from browse to order.
