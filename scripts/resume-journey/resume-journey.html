01 · Origins · 1984–1992

Where curiosity begins.

Born in 1984, I began preschool in 1988. My first introduction to computing came in 1992, with a 486 PC, DOS and WordStar—the beginning of my curiosity about technology.

  • 1984 Born
  • 1988 Started preschool
  • 1992 First computing experience · 486 PC, DOS & WordStar
02 · Computing foundation · 1994

Learning the logic.

In 1994, I explored Windows, FoxPro, COBOL and GW-BASIC, building on my early computing experience and beginning to understand how software works.

  • Desktop computing Windows
  • Data & programming FoxPro & COBOL
  • Programming fundamentals GW-BASIC
03 · Design discovery · 1998

Creativity meets technology.

In 1998, desktop publishing became my bridge between creativity and technology. I explored CorelDRAW, Photoshop and PageMaker, began learning Turbo C and C++, and created small projects for schools and colleges.

  • Graphic design CorelDRAW & Photoshop
  • Layout & publishing PageMaker
  • Programming Turbo C & C++
  • Early projects Small projects for schools & colleges
04 · Education & clients · 2001

Learning through real work.

In 2001, after completing school, I began an M.Sc. in Software Engineering. Alongside my studies, I created marketing materials and brand collateral for corporate clients.

  • Education Began M.Sc. in Software Engineering
  • Marketing design Marketing materials
  • Brand identity Brand collateral for corporate clients
05 · Professional launch · 2006

Design goes digital.

In 2006, I completed my M.Sc. in Software Engineering and joined Olympic Media, an advertising agency. My work expanded from graphic design into web experiences, laying the foundation for my move into UX and UI design.

  • Education Completed M.Sc. in Software Engineering
  • Organization Joined Olympic Media
  • Digital design Graphic design & web experiences
  • Career direction Transition toward UX/UI design
06 · Enterprise UX · 2014

Designing larger systems.

In 2014, I joined Zensar as a Senior UX/UI Designer, expanding my work into enterprise experiences and complex digital systems.

  • Organization Joined Zensar
  • Role Senior UX/UI Designer
  • Design focus Enterprise experiences
  • Product complexity Complex digital systems
07 · Leadership & BFSI · 2018

Leading at greater scale.

In 2018, I moved on-site to lead a larger design team for a banking, financial services and insurance (BFSI) client. My work brought together team leadership, complex financial systems and stakeholder needs.

  • Team leadership Led a larger on-site design team
  • Domain Banking, financial services & insurance
  • Design focus Complex financial systems
  • Collaboration Stakeholder alignment
08 · AI & the future · 2026

Building what’s next.

In 2026, I returned to India and joined Accurate Background as Product Design Lead. Alongside this new role, I began an MBA focused on AI, continuing my growth in product design leadership and emerging technology.

  • Location Returned to India
  • Organization Accurate Background
  • Role Product Design Lead
  • Learning MBA focused on AI

The journey continues—with new challenges, continued learning and the next chapter in product design.
