ZapPay: Mobile Payments & Financial Transactions Platform

A 0-to-1 mobile payments platform designed to make everyday transactions fast, secure, and reliable.

+24% successful transaction ratesQR, P2P & bill payments

The
Problem

Confusion across multi-step payment flows made QR, peer-to-peer, and bill payments feel uncertain.

  • Payment journeys were fragmented across multiple transaction types
  • Users lacked clear status and feedback during critical steps
  • Trust depended on knowing what the system was doing and what would happen next

Key
Insights

Users did not struggle with features; they struggled with fragmented flows and missing feedback that made financial actions feel risky.

  • Trust grows from clear status and feedback loops
  • Users expect fast, predictable, low-effort transactions
  • Financial actions need reassurance throughout the journey, not just at the end
  • Reducing steps is not enough; clarity at every step matters

My Role &
Ownership

Led UX strategy and end-to-end product design across core transaction journeys.

  • Aligned product experience with business goals
  • Designed end-to-end systems across onboarding, payments, and wallet experiences
  • Created scalable design patterns and interaction models
  • Partnered with product, engineering, and stakeholders through delivery

Constraints &
Complexity

  • Support multiple payment modes including QR, P2P, cards, and wallets
  • Balance flexibility with simplicity across payment flows
  • Design for varied user behaviors and levels of financial literacy
  • Maintain consistency across fragmented features and journeys

Key Product &
UX Decisions

  • Simplified onboarding with progressive disclosure and contextual inputs
  • Introduced a unified home dashboard for faster access to key actions
  • Designed modular transaction flows for multiple payment types
  • Embedded real-time system feedback throughout transaction steps
  • Standardized interaction patterns to reduce the learning curve
  • Improved feature discovery through structured information architecture and navigation

Tradeoffs &
Decisions

  • Used progressive onboarding instead of immediate data capture to reduce drop-offs
  • Balanced multiple payment paths with guided user flows
  • Prioritized clarity and feedback over minimal interface density
  • Standardized flows while preserving room for payment-specific needs
  • Optimized for speed while keeping security cues visible
