HomeNest: Smart Living & Home Automation

A unified smart home experience for monitoring, controlling, and automating connected devices.

Device controlReal-time feedback

The
Problem

People struggled to monitor and control connected devices across fragmented controls, unclear system states, and weak real-time feedback.

  • Device controls were disconnected across environments
  • System status and performance were difficult to see
  • Everyday interactions varied across devices

Key
Insights

Users needed to understand what the home system was doing and see the result of every action.

  • Immediate feedback builds trust and control
  • Frequent interactions should stay simple
  • Clear hierarchy improves low-attention control tasks

My Role &
Ownership

Led UX strategy and end-to-end product design for a multi-device smart home platform.

  • Defined the direction for home-control experiences
  • Designed device interaction patterns and mobile interfaces
  • Built scalable patterns for multi-device environments
  • Partnered with product and engineering

Constraints &
Complexity

  • Handle multiple device states and control types
  • Balance dark-interface aesthetics with usability
  • Keep the experience responsive across home environments
  • Maintain consistency across device ecosystems

Key Product &
UX Decisions

  • Introduced a centralized control dashboard
  • Made on, off, active, and idle states explicit
  • Added immediate feedback for device interactions
  • Simplified frequent control flows
  • Prioritized key controls through visual hierarchy

User Flow
Transformation

Unified room selection, device status, and everyday controls into a consistent monitoring and control journey.

Component Thinking

  • Standardized system-state indicators
  • Consistent feedback and transitions across devices
  • Scalable layouts for new environments and integrations
