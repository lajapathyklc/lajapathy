Eater: Food Discovery & Ordering

A refreshed food-ordering experience focused on easier discovery, faster decisions, and a smoother path from browsing to checkout.

Food discoveryOrdering & checkout

PROJECT SNAPSHOT

ROLE
Designed food discovery, browsing, and checkout flows
SCOPE
Category navigation · Product selection · Order review
DOMAIN
Food discovery & ordering · Mobile commerce
CHALLENGE
Too many food choices and unclear categories slowed decisions, while uneven filters and checkout made orders harder to complete.
KEY DECISION
Organized browsing around clear food categories and standardized interactions through checkout.
IMPACT
Improved food discovery through structured categories

The
Problem

People struggled to discover, compare, and order food efficiently in a cluttered experience with too many choices and inconsistent navigation.

  • Food discovery was fragmented across categories
  • Too many options slowed decisions
  • Filtering and checkout patterns were inconsistent
  • Recommendations lacked context and personalization

Key
Insights

The issue was not access to food options; it was making confident decisions among overwhelming choices.

  • Guided discovery works better than endless browsing
  • Visual hierarchy helps people choose faster
  • Contextual recommendations can improve relevance
  • Shorter ordering flows support conversion
