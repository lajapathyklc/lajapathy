Amazon: Product Discovery & Checkout

A concept redesign focused on making product discovery clearer and the checkout journey simpler.

Product discoveryCheckout experience

PROJECT SNAPSHOT

ROLE
Lead Product Designer
SCOPE
Product discovery · Product evaluation · Checkout
DOMAIN
E-commerce · Shopping concept
CHALLENGE
The concept aimed to help shoppers find and choose products, then move from cart to checkout with less effort.
KEY DECISION
Kept key product information visible during evaluation and carried consistent patterns through checkout.
IMPACT
Concept focused on improved product discovery and a simpler checkout journey

Project
Focus

The concept explores the core shopping journey from finding the right product to completing a purchase.

  • Make product discovery easier to scan
  • Help shoppers move from options to a confident choice
  • Reduce friction between the cart and checkout

Design
Approach

  • Clarify the path from search and browsing to product details
  • Keep key product information visible during evaluation
  • Make cart review and purchase actions easier to follow
  • Carry consistent patterns through checkout
